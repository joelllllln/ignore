How to play

  • The game opens up as you go. Only what you can actually use is on screen — COLLECTORS, skill trees, abilities and ASCENSION each appear the first time they matter, and a one-line objective above the shop always names your next move. Nothing here is missing; it is just waiting its turn.
  • Defenders auto-fire at dots. Dots are TANKY — more damage = faster kills = more income, so your defenders & their skill trees ARE your economy.
  • Armored dots (double-ringed) have huge defense and move slowly — they need real firepower but drop a big bounty, and their cash orbs are heavy & slow, so you'll want stronger collectors.
  • Every planet has its own native enemy race with a unique ability & look — splitters that split again and again, shielded bastions, cloakers, teleporting wraiths, loot-eating devourers, minion-spawning broods and more. The tougher a dot, the more spikes & rings it grows. Tap a planet on the map to see its native race.
  • The field has no edges — dots come in over the edge of your view, all the way around, and your army holds the middle. The field is the whole screen: the menu sits on top of it, so Minimise uncovers more of the same planet rather than changing it. Pinch to zoom right out and see twice the field; nothing marks where it ends.
  • Big kills drop heavy loot that a collector must sit and consume (watch the ring fill) — small dots are instant, fat ones tie a collector up.
  • Tap any defender (or hit ⬆ Tree) to open its skill tree — an interconnected node map. Allocate outward from the centre; each node needs a connected node first. Nodes stack additively (each adds a flat bonus that sums — linear, no runaway; early nodes feel strong, later ones taper), and ✦ keystones add multishot AND a crazy weapon specialization — ✦Explosive Rounds (bombs), ✦Chain Lightning, or ✦Piercing Laser. A whole ◈ Mind branch upgrades the unit's intelligence & coordination — smart units avoid overkill, don't both shoot the same doomed dot, and triage the most valuable targets, so no shot is wasted. Node prices grow with DEPTH — each ring outward costs more, but buying a node never raises any other node’s price, so any node you can afford is always a good buy. Every unit of that class shares the tree.
  • Collectors gather the cash dots drop. Buy more in the COLLECTORS tab and unlock new classes across all three systems: Drone → Drone Swarm → Heavy Collector → Magnet → Tractor → Black Hole → Wormhole (the last two slowly drag everything in). Each has its own skill web.
  • Conquer planets to unlock Travel and add each planet to your idle empire (it keeps earning for you forever, even offline). Your whole army travels with you — units, trees, upgrades and cash all carry forward; nothing restarts. Each new planet's dots are tougher and richer, and every conquer bar takes roughly ×1.65 as long as the last, so each campaign eventually slows into a wall. That's by design: it's your cue to Ascend. Conquest rewards playing: your empire banks cash around the clock (away a week, earn a week) and chips the conquer bar for you — but only at 20% of active pace, so being at the controls is ~5× faster and no planet falls while you're away.
  • A conquered planet is SETTLED — at peace, no more enemies. It does not pay you rent for owning it: what it pays is a one-time pot of ⚑ victory spoils, paid out fast while you're parked there (easily covers your next launch), and once that pot is spent the planet is done paying. Income comes from playing, never from holding — the frontier is the only place money is made, and you can put a ⛏ ◈ CORE MINE on it — and that comes with the planet, because filling the conquer bar does not take a planet, it earns you the right to fight for it. At 100% the planet is still contested: keep farming and banking as long as you like, and when you are ready the dock button reads ▲ SUMMON and calls out that planet’s own ▲ SEAM KEEPER. Every planet has a different one, named and built from its native race’s tricks (brood-floods, reflecting shields, cloakers, blinkers, loot-eaters…). Beat it inside 60s and the planet is yours — the conquest plays out and its hoard founds the ◈ core mine on the spot. Lose and nothing is lost: the bar stays full, you keep farming, and you can call it out again as often as you like. There is no way past the keeper — no planet is conquered, and no mine is ever founded, without beating it. Once built, the panel shows the mine at work with a countdown to the next ◈ — every mine digs on its own independent timeline, even while you're away — P1 pays 1◈ every couple of days and each deeper mine runs ×1.3 faster, adding up to roughly a quarter of the ◈ a run banks. Conquering is still the main payday, but a well-built empire of mines is a real second stream.
  • ASCENSION — the long game. Each planet's conquer bar takes ~×1.65 as long as the last, so every run eventually hits a WALL. Ascend — the game's ONLY reset: the cluster resets, every conquered planet banks a handful of ◈ cores (deeper = more), and cores buy one permanent thing — the Singularity Engine: +25% ALL income per level — that makes the next run melt your old territory. THE LADDER: hop at ~3 planets on your first run, then ride every ascension a few planets deeper — when the ◈ button burns amber, the wall is here and hopping beats grinding. Planet 18 is the summit of many ascensions.
  • ECONOMY tab boosts cash value, spawn rate, capacity & luck. Every level is one continuous ladder for the whole campaign — nothing resets between planets; each level simply costs more than the last, and only Ascension starts the ladder fresh.
  • Tap the planet bar (top) for the star map — three solar systems of planets. Travel to new planets for serious money: a fresh planet greets you with few but genuinely tough dots dropping fat bounties — and the field grows thicker and meaner as your conquest advances. Tough fields pay a premium, so pushing the bar is always where the money is.
  • Draw across the field to blast dots yourself — finger kills CHAIN into a COMBO: the quicker you kill, the bigger the bounty multiplier, up to ×5 (stop slashing and it drains away). Your draw also hits mini-bosses for DOUBLE, so actively drawing is how you seal a boss kill before its timer runs out.
